Tapzilla Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Tapzilla stores game progress, preferences, achievements, and purchase status on your device. Tapzilla does not require an account, and the developer does not receive this gameplay information directly.

Advertising and data processing

Tapzilla displays advertising using Google Mobile Ads and manages applicable privacy choices using Google’s User Messaging Platform. Depending on your location, device settings, consent choices, and the developer’s AdMob configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process approximate location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data. These data may be used to deliver and measure advertising, provide analytics, prevent fraud, and maintain advertising services.

Tapzilla requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads may still use device information and contextual signals.

Your privacy choices

Where required, you can review or change available advertising privacy choices in Tapzilla under Settings > Ad Privacy Choices. You can also purchase Remove Ads to disable advertising in the app.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases under Apple’s privacy policy. Tapzilla receives only the purchase entitlement needed to unlock the Remove Ads feature.

Third-party services

  • Apple Privacy Policy — App Store and StoreKit

Children

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and privacy obligations depend on the intended audience and account configuration. Parents or guardians may contact the developer using the address below.
